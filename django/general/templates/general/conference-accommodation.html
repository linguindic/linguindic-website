Accommodation

The conference will take place at Wolfson College, Linton Rd, Oxford, OX2 6UD. The college has a small number of rooms that may be available, from £72 to £93 per night, using the following link: https://www.wolfson.ox.ac.uk/bed-breakfast/

Hotels nearby include:

Linton Lodge BW Signature Collection Hotel (from £170 per night)

Cotswold Lodge Hotel (from £185 per night)

100 Banbury Road Oxford (from £160 per night)

The Easyhotel in Summertown is cheaper (from £100 per night); there are frequent buses between Summertown and Linton Road.

Hotels further out are cheaper, but these are two bus rides away from Linton Road:

Premier Inn Oxford Botley hotel (from £77 per night)

Travelodge Oxford Abingdon Road (from £80 per night)

In the City Centre: Central Backpackers (from £76 per night)

Other affordable options may be available here: https://www.universityrooms.com

Transportation

From London to Oxford:

To travel from London airports to Oxford, you can take a train (from Heathrow, Stansted, or Gatwick) or a bus/coach (from Heathrow, Gatwick, Stansted, or Luton). Further information can be found here: https://www.theairlineoxford.co.uk/, https://www.nationalexpress.com, https://www.thetrainline.com/

To travel from London to Oxford, you can take a direct train from London Paddington or London Marylebone, or a bus with the Oxford Tube (24h) or National Express. Further information can be found here: https://www.oxfordtube.com/, https://www.nationalexpress.com, https://www.thetrainline.com/

In Oxford:

You can travel using the local buses: https://www.oxfordbus.co.uk/ or https://www.stagecoachbus.com/ You can pay with a contactless card, cash or a bus card: single ticket from £2.50, same day return from £4.50, there are other options available in their website.

Wolfson College also has a minibus service free of charge. The minibus runs from the college to the city centre and back, you can find the schedules (and even track it) in the following link: https://www.wolfson.ox.ac.uk/minibus-2/
